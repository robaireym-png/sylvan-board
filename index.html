How to use this board (2-minute read)

What each column means

  • Ideas — "maybe someday." Nobody is working on these. Parking them here keeps them from getting lost.
  • Backlog — real work we plan to do, just not this week. Ordered roughly by when it's needed.
  • Next Up (Sprint) — what we've committed to finish this sprint. Top card = most important.
  • In Development — someone is actively working on it right now.
  • Review / Waiting on Robert — finished work for you to check, or a decision only you can make. Nothing behind these cards can move until you answer.
  • Done — finished and signed off by you.

What a sprint is

A sprint is a short, fixed block of time (here: one week, Thursday to Wednesday) with a small list of work we promise to finish. Short sprints make it obvious, every week, whether we're on track for Oct 26. If something doesn't fit, it goes back to Backlog instead of quietly slipping.

Current sprint: Sprint 1, Oct 1 – Oct 7. Goal: barracks finished and on your phone, plus the decisions that unblock it.

How to move cards

Tap ◀ or ▶ on a card to move it one column left or right. Your moves are saved on this device only. To make a move permanent for everyone, just tell Claude ("move X to Done" or "answer to X: yes") and the board gets republished.

Suggested weekly routine (about 15 minutes)

  • Wednesday evening — review: look at Review / Waiting on Robert. Answer the top 2–3 decisions (one line each is enough). Move anything you've approved to Done.
  • Thursday — plan the sprint: pick the next few cards from Backlog into Next Up. Rule of thumb: no more than 6–8 cards in a sprint.
  • Daily, optional — 1-minute glance: is anything stuck in In Development or waiting on you for more than two days? Say so.
  • End of each sprint: ask "are we still on track for Oct 26?" If not, cut scope, not quality.
