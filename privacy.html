Sylvan & Scourge - Privacy Policy

Effective 10 October 2026. Applies to the Sylvan & Scourge app for iPhone and iPad.

Sylvan & Scourge is an idle strategy game made by an independent developer. We collect as little as we can. We do not sell your data, we do not show third-party ads, and we do not use advertising or tracking tools.

1. What stays on your device

Your game progress (resources, buildings, troops, settings) is saved on your device. We cannot see it.

2. Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, the app reads your Game Center player ID and display name so it can show your name on scores. Apple handles Game Center sign-in under Apple's privacy policy. If you are not signed in, you play as "Guest".

3. Leaderboards

When online leaderboards are switched on, the app sends your display name, a player ID and your event scores to our game server so scores can be ranked. Nothing else is sent: no contacts, location, photos or device identifiers for advertising. Scores may be removed when an event ends.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. The app only learns which item you bought so it can give it to you.

5. Beta testing

If you test the game through Apple TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and the feedback you choose to send with us, under Apple's TestFlight terms. We use them only to fix the game.

6. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

7. Your choices

  • Sign out of Game Center to play without a name on scores.
  • Deleting the app deletes your saved game from your device.
  • To have your leaderboard name and scores removed, email us and we will delete them.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
